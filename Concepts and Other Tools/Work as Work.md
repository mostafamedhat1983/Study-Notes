- **Work as Work**: Treating all work needed to build, run, secure, and improve a product as one shared backlog.

## Why it matters

Separating feature work from operational work hides important work and creates competing priorities. A shared backlog helps teams understand the full effort required to deliver a reliable product.

## Work categories

Common categories are:

- Features
- Operational work
- Technical debt
- Compliance and risk work
- Unplanned work

## How to apply it

- Make all work visible in one backlog.
- Use a shared prioritization process.
- Estimate the impact of delaying operational work.
- Track the ratio of work across categories.
- Review product-health data to check whether priorities are effective.

## Product-health data

Useful signals include incident trends, error rates, crash rates, API response time, Mean Time to Detect (MTTD), and Mean Time to Restore (MTTR).

## Implementation approach

Start with a small pilot team. Define a measurable target, review progress at checkpoints, and adjust before expanding the approach.

## Key idea

Feature delivery and operational reliability are shared product responsibilities, not separate types of work owned by separate teams.
