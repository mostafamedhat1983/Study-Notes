- **Westrum Organizational Culture**: A model that describes how culture affects information flow, collaboration, and organizational performance. DevOps aims for a generative culture.

## Culture types

| Culture | Main focus | Information flow | Response to failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pathological** | Power and self-protection | Information is hidden, distorted, or withheld | Blame and scapegoating |
| **Bureaucratic** | Rules and departmental boundaries | Information moves through formal processes | Handled through procedures |
| **Generative** | Mission and performance | Information is shared quickly and openly | Inquiry, learning, and improvement |

## Generative culture behaviors

- Teams cooperate across organizational boundaries.
- People are encouraged to report problems and risks.
- Responsibility and risk are shared.
- Bridging silos is encouraged.
- Failures lead to learning instead of blame.
- New ideas are welcomed and tested.

## Why it matters in DevOps

Good information flow helps teams find issues early, make better decisions, collaborate across teams, and continuously improve.
