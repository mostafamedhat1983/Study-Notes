- **A3 Problem Solving**: A structured Lean method for understanding, solving, and learning from problems. The name comes from the A3 paper size often used to document the analysis.

## Steps

1. **Background**: Explain why the problem matters and its business or customer impact.
2. **Current condition**: Describe the current situation using data.
3. **Target condition**: Define the measurable result to achieve.
4. **Analysis**: Identify the contributing factors. Complex problems can have multiple causes.
5. **Countermeasures**: Propose changes or experiments and state the expected result.
6. **Implementation plan**: Define actions, owners, timelines, and measurements.
7. **Standardize and follow up**: If a countermeasure works, make it standard work. Otherwise, learn and adjust.

## Relation to PDCA

- Steps 1 to 4: **Plan**
- Step 5: **Do**
- Step 6: **Check**
- Step 7: **Adjust**

## DevOps uses

Use A3 thinking to investigate problems such as excessive unplanned work, workload imbalance, repeated incidents, slow delivery, or missing feedback loops.

For example, measure how much work is interrupted by incidents, identify the causes, test countermeasures, and standardize what works.

## Key idea

Spend most of the effort understanding the problem before implementing a solution.
